Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 8, 2026

1. General Provisions

This Privacy Policy (hereinafter referred to as the "Policy") defines the procedure for processing and protecting personal data of users of the BilateralBound website (hereinafter referred to as the "Site"), located at https://emdrbilateral.ru/.

By using the Site, you agree to the terms of this Policy. If you do not agree, please stop using the Site.

Site Administrator: Bugaev David.
Contact email: davidbugayov@ya.ru

2. What Data We Collect

2.1. Automatically Collected Data

When visiting the Site, the following information is automatically collected:

  • IP address;
  • browser type and version;
  • device operating system;
  • date and time of visit;
  • pages viewed by the user;
  • referrer source;
  • unique device identifier (cookie).

This data is collected using Yandex.Metrica (YANDEX LLC, 119021, Russia, Moscow, 16 Lva Tolstogo St.).

2.2. EMDR Therapy Session Data

When creating an EMDR therapy session, the Site generates a unique session identifier. This identifier is stored temporarily in the server's RAM and does not contain personal data. The identifier is automatically deleted when the session ends or after a period of inactivity.

The Site does not collect:

  • names, surnames or other identifying data;
  • email addresses (except when contacting support);
  • phone numbers;
  • payment information;
  • health data.

3. Cookies

The Site uses cookies for analytics (Yandex.Metrica). Cookies are small text files saved on your device.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings or use the cookie consent banner on the Site. Disabling cookies may affect some Site features.

On your first visit to the Site, a banner will appear requesting consent to use cookies and analytics. You can change your choice at any time.

4. Purposes of Data Processing

Collected data is used for:

  • analysis of traffic and user behavior on the Site (Yandex.Metrica);
  • improving the quality and functionality of the Site;
  • providing technical support and security;
  • responding to user inquiries.

Personal data is not used for:

  • targeted advertising;
  • transfer to third parties (except as required by law);
  • profiling and automated decision-making.

5. Legal Basis for Processing

Processing of personal data is carried out on the basis of:

  • Federal Law No. 152-FZ of July 27, 2006 "On Personal Data";
  • user consent expressed through the use of the Site and/or the cookie consent banner.

6. Data Transfer to Third Parties

Personal data is transferred only to Yandex.Metrica for analytics purposes. Yandex.Metrica processes data in accordance with its Privacy Policy.

In other cases, data is not transferred to third parties, except as provided by the legislation of the Russian Federation.

7. Data Storage

Analytics data (Yandex.Metrica) is stored in accordance with the terms set by Yandex.Metrica.

EMDR therapy session identifiers are stored temporarily in the server's RAM and are automatically deleted:

  • when the session ends;
  • after 24 hours of session inactivity;
  • when the server is restarted.

8. User Rights

In accordance with Federal Law No. 152-FZ, you have the right to:

  • obtain information about the processing of your personal data;
  • request clarification, blocking or destruction of data;
  • withdraw consent to data processing;
  • appeal the operator's actions to the authorized body (Roskomnadzor).

To exercise your rights, contact us via email: davidbugayov@ya.ru.

9. Yandex.Metrica and Web Analytics

The Site uses Yandex.Metrica, a web analytics service provided by YANDEX LLC. Yandex.Metrica uses cookies and collects data about user behavior. Information about the use of the Site is transmitted to Yandex and processed in accordance with Yandex's Privacy Policy.

You can disable data collection by Yandex.Metrica using the official opt-out tool.

10. Data Security

The Site Administrator takes necessary organizational and technical measures to protect personal data from unlawful or accidental access, destruction, modification, blocking, copying, distribution, as well as from other unlawful actions of third parties.

11. Changes to the Policy

The Site Administrator reserves the right to make changes to this Policy. The new version of the Policy takes effect upon its publication on the Site. We recommend periodically checking the relevance of the Policy.

12. Contact Information

For all questions related to the processing of personal data, please contact:

Email: davidbugayov@ya.ru
GitHub: github.com/davidbugayov

13. Legal Status and Data Operator Registry

The Site Administrator is an individual (self-employed) and is not a legal entity. INN, OGRN, and KPP do not apply.

In accordance with paragraph 1 of part 2 of Article 22 of Federal Law No. 152-FZ "On Personal Data", the administrator is exempt from the obligation to notify Roskomnadzor about the processing of personal data, since personal data is processed exclusively to ensure the operation of the site and does not involve the transfer of data to third parties (except Yandex.Metrica).

Registration in the register of personal data operators (Roskomnadzor) is not required.
