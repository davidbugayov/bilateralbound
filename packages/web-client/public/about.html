About EMDR Therapy & BilateralBound

What is EMDR Therapy?

EMDR (Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing) is an evidence-based psychotherapy developed by Dr. Francine Shapiro in 1987. It is recognized by the WHO and APA as a first-line treatment for PTSD.

During EMDR, the patient focuses on a traumatic memory while experiencing bilateral stimulation — typically eye movements. This dual-attention process helps the brain reprocess the traumatic memory.

Bilateral stimulation mimics the natural processing that occurs during REM sleep, allowing the brain to integrate traumatic memories.

What is Bilateral Stimulation?

Bilateral stimulation (BLS) is the rhythmic, alternating activation of the left and right brain hemispheres:

  • Visual: following a moving object with the eyes (primary method used by BilateralBound)
  • Auditory: alternating sounds in the left and right ear
  • Tactile: alternating taps or vibrations

What Conditions Does EMDR Treat?

  • Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD) and Complex PTSD
  • Anxiety disorders and panic attacks
  • Depression
  • Phobias and obsessive-compulsive disorder (OCD)
  • Grief and loss
  • Relationship trauma and couples therapy

How BilateralBound Works

  1. Therapist creates a session on the main page. A unique session ID is generated.
  2. Patient connects via a viewer link on any device — no installation needed.
  3. Real-time bilateral stimulation — therapist controls a moving ball, patient follows with eyes.
  4. Full session control — speed, direction, ball size, color, and bilateral audio.
