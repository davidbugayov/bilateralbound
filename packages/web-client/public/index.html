How to Use the Online EMDR Tool

🎯 What is EMDR Therapy?

EMDR (Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing) is a scientifically validated psychotherapy method for treating post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD), anxiety disorders, and psychological trauma.

Our platform uses bilateral stimulation — the ball movement creates bilateral brain stimulation, helping to process traumatic memories.

✅ Indications for use:

  • Post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD)
  • Anxiety disorders and panic attacks
  • Depression and emotional trauma
  • Phobias and obsessive states
  • Couple therapy and family conflicts

⚠️ Important to remember:

EMDR therapy should only be performed by qualified professionals. This platform is intended for use in professional psychotherapeutic practice.
